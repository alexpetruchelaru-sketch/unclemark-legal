Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how the Uncle Mark publishing tool ("the app", "we") handles information when it connects to social media accounts. The app is a private, self-hosted tool used by Uncle Mark to plan, schedule and publish content to accounts that Uncle Mark owns. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When an account owner connects an account and grants permission, the app may access:

  • Basic profile information: account name, username, profile picture and account identifier.
  • Publishing permissions: the ability to upload and publish photos and videos to the connected account.
  • Account and content statistics: follower counts, views, likes and similar metrics for the connected account and its own posts.
  • Access tokens issued by the platform (TikTok, YouTube/Google, Instagram/Meta) so the app can act on the account's behalf.

We only request the permissions needed to schedule, publish and measure content for the connected account.

How we use it

  • To publish content the account owner has prepared, at the time they choose.
  • To show the account owner their own account and post performance.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the app for the account owner.

Storage and sharing

Information is stored on a server operated by Uncle Mark. We do not sell, rent or share it with third parties, except with the platform itself when publishing (for example, sending a video to TikTok or YouTube) and where required by law.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while an account is connected. You can remove the app's access at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites.

You can also email us to request deletion of any stored data, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is not used to collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
